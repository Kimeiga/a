How it works

Points are everything

Hits pay 10, body kills 60, headshots 100, a knife kill 130. Points buy doors, weapons, perks and Pack‑A‑Punch. Shooting a zombie a few times before killing it is worth more than one-shotting it — that is the whole economy.

The loop

Open doors to reach the theater, find the power switch in the power room to wake the perk machines and the teleporter, then link the mainframe in the lobby and teleport from the stage to Pack‑A‑Punch your weapon.

Train them

They walk straight at you and they never stop. The theater floor is big enough to run a circle and gather the whole round into one train behind you. That is the skill.

Watch for

Crawlers burst into Nova‑6 gas. Hounds arrive in whole rounds and are faster than you. Screechers fly, so the ground is not where you should be looking. Spitters shoot you from across the room. A Panzer shows up when it feels like it.

Settings

tuning is in-memory only

Medium adds cast shadows from the room light, High adds the wet lobby marble. Both cost fill rate, which is what a phone runs out of first.

Auto watches the frame time and moves the render scale to hold the rate. It is the setting to leave alone.
